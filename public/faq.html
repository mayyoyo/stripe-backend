Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our mobile notary services

What is a mobile notary?

A mobile notary travels to your location to notarize documents, providing convenience and saving you time.

How much does a general notary cost?

Our general notary fee is $10 per signature.

Do you handle loan signings?

Yes! We are certified Loan Signing Agents and charge $125–$175 depending on the package.

Can you visit hospitals or jails?

Absolutely. We charge a flat fee of $50 plus travel fees for hospital, jail, or home visits.

Are there travel fees?

Yes, travel fees are based on distance:

  • 0–10 miles: $25
  • 11–20 miles: $40
  • 21–30 miles: $60

Do you charge convenience fees?

Convenience fees apply for non-standard hours:

  • Evenings: +$20
  • Weekends: +$25
  • After 9PM: +$50

What is your cancellation policy?

20% deposit is required to secure your appointment:

  • Cancel 24+ hrs before: No fee
  • Cancel 12–24 hrs before: 50% fee
  • Cancel less than 12 hrs: Deposit non-refundable

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online via our booking page or call us directly at 703-967-0674.
